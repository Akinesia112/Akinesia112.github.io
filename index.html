About Myself

Hi! I'm a Master's student in Prof. Bing-Yu "Robin" Chen's group at National Taiwan University (CSIE, AI Program) 🇹🇼. My research lies at Sim–Real Transfer: the intersection of AI / Graphics / Robotics/ Audio/ HPC, with specializations in Physics-informed Machine Learning, Reinforcement Learning, and Graph Learning.

Research Areas

I like systematically working through critical problems that sit between disciplines, and my current interests span three core areas:

  • Visual Computing & Physics Simulation — developing neural PDE solvers (e.g., Walk-on-Spheres Monte Carlo for complex geometry) and differentiable physical simulation frameworks (hyper-elastic projective dynamics with positive-definite Hessian projection), with applications spanning CAD-driven robotic fabrication and animation workflows such as editable 3D Gaussian Splatting.

  • Embodied Systems — training Sim2Real reinforcement learning policies in Isaac Gym/Lab for robotic manipulation on Kuka, UR10, and Hiwin arms, and building large-scale digital twins that fuse multimodal sensing (WiFi, point cloud, RGBD) with graph federated learning foundation models for privacy-preserving human–scene interaction on supercomputers like Fugaku.

  • Audio Signal Processing — combining classical DSP with learned latent-space representations to design audio watermarks robust to neural resynthesis, and modeling spatial audio through room impulse responses (RIRs) in complex room acoustics.
